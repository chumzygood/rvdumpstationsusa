                                      It has a one lane boat ramp and shoreline to fish from. There is no camping allowed
                                      in this park. Big Sandy Access is a free access point with a two lane boat ramp and shoreline to fish
                                      from. There is no camping allowed at this access point. It is located off of Shady
                                      Shores Road in Shady Shores. Hickory Creek Park 1201 Point Vista Rd. Hickory Creek TX 75065 Hickory Creek is a Class A campground located in Hickory Creek, Texas on Lewisville
                                        Lake. Hickory Creek Park Amenities: Camping sites with water and electrical hookups, picnic tables, grills and fire rings ($30 per night) Boatramp (for campers only) Restrooms with showers Dump station Shoreline access for fishing Walking/Biking trail Gate attendant on duty during open hours, 10am-10pm Take a virtual tour of Hickory Creek Park. Use Fees: 50 amp and water campsites are $30/night Golden Age, Golden Access and America the Beautiful Senior and Disability pass holders receive 50% off the
                                          regular fees. Meet your Park Hosts: In order to make your stay more enjoyable, Park Hosts are on site to answer your questions, remind you of park rules and
                                          hand out goodies for the kids! Reservations: Campsites are available for use through reservations made at www.recreation.gov . (opens in new website) Rules and Regs: The park is closed between 10 pm and 6 am. Entry into the park is not permitted during
                                          those times. Day users are not permitted in the campground. A full copy of the
                                          Rules and Regulations can be found under Recreation Rules and Regulations tab. Directions: The park is located off I-35 South (also known as Stemmons Freeway) .  After
                                          exiting the FM 2181/Swisher Rd exit (Exit 458) off the freeway, turn right onto
                                          Turbeville Road and then left on Point Vista Road.  The park will be on your
                                          right.  Hickory Creek is located close to restaurants, a movie theater, and
                                          grocery shopping. A map can be found here . Amenities: Picnic tables with grills Four pavilions 2 Restrooms Shoreline access for fishing 1 Boat ramps with loading docks Long parking spaces for boat trailers Gate attendant controlled access Use Fees: Day Use fees are $5
                                      a vehicle Pavilions: Four open sided pavilions are available in the park.  Each pavilion is
                                      within walking distance of a bathroom and has a large grill, water and electric hook-up. Rules and Regs: Westlake is open 6 am to 10 pm year round. Entry into the parks is not permitted outside
                                        those times. A full copy of the Rules and Regulations can be found under Recreation
                                        Rules and Regulations tab. Directions: On I-35E going North or South take the Swisher Exit (exit 458). From the North cross
                                      over I-35 onto Lake Dallas Dr. Stay on Lake Dallas Dr. until the Main St. stopsign.
                                      Turn left and stay on Main St. until it enters into the parks. From the South, take the Lake Dallas Dr road until Main St stop sign. Turn right and
