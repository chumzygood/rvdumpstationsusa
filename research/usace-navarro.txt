                                                            Grazing Leases Water Safety Natural Resources Invasive Species Cultural Resources FAQ Links Corps Parks at Navarro Mills Lake There are five developed park areas at Lake Navarro Mills. The available facilities are described in each park section: Liberty Hill Park Located in north central Texas, Liberty Hill Park is located on the southwestern   shore of Navarro Mills Lake. The park is located just north of Dawson, Texas and can be accessed by travelling about 4 miles north of Dawson on FM 709. Liberty   Hill Park offers 99 campsites with water and electricity.  Five of the sites have   screen shelters, fourteen have sewer hookups, and there are three primitive campsites with no hookups. There   are two boat ramps, a fishing dock, marina, playground and swim beach in the park. For more detailed information, you may visit the Developed Camping Areas page. Important Information The   gatehouse is located at the park entrance and is open for business between 6:00   a.m. and 10:00 p.m. The gate attendants live on site and are available in cases   of emergencies 24 hours per day. All user fees are collected at the   gatehouse. Additional Restrictions Pets must be kept on leash while in the park. Vehicles are restricted to authorized roadways or parking areas only. Off-road vehicles may not be operated on any of the government property at Navarro Mills Lake. Fires are restricted to designated areas only. There are no picnic sites in Liberty Hill Park. A camping permit must be purchased to occupy any of the sites. The   following fees are only charged to members of the public who have not paid the   standard camping fees. Boat Launch Fee $5.00 Dump Station Fee $4.00 Swimming (per   person) $2.00 Wolf Creek Park Located in north central Texas, Wolf Creek Park is located on the north shore of   Navarro Mills Lake. Wolf Creek Park offers 50 campsites with water and electric   service, 20 non-electric campsites, 2 non-electric double campsites, public   restrooms and showers, group shelter, fishing pier and a two lane boat ramp with a courtesy dock. The park is open from April 1st through September 30th each year and can be reached by travelling about three miles north on FM 667 from the intersection with state highway 31 to FM 639. Turn west (left) on FM 639 and go about two miles to Wolf Creek Park (FM 639 ends at Wolf Creek Park entrance). For more detailed information, you may visit the Developed Camping Areas page. Important Information The   park is open from April 1 through September 30. The gatehouse is located at the   park entrance and is open for business between 6:00 am and 10:00 p.m. The gate   attendants live on site and are available in case of emergencies 24 hours per   day. All user fees are collected at the gatehouse. Additional Restrictions Pets must be kept on leash while in the park. Vehicles are restricted to authorized roadways or parking areas only. Off-road vehicles may not be operated on any of the government property at Navarro Mills Lake. Fires are restricted to designated areas only. There are no picnic sites in Liberty Hill Park. A camping permit must be purchased to occupy any of the sites. The   following fees are only charged to members of the public who have not paid the   standard camping fees. Boat Launch Fee $5.00 Dump Station Fee $4.00 Oak Park Located in north central Texas, Oak Park is located on the northeastern shore of   Navarro Mills Lake. Oak Park offers 48 campsites with water and electric   service, a group shelter, designated swimming area and playground, fishing pier,   public restrooms and a boat ramp. There is also a separate day-use area open   April - September with picnic tables, restrooms, and a swimming beach. For more detailed information, you may visit the Developed Camping Areas page. Important Information The   gatehouse is located at the park entrance and is open for business between 6:00   a.m. and 10:00 p.m. The gate attendants live on site and are available in cases   of emergencies 24 hours per day. All user fees are collected at the   gatehouse. Additional Restrictions Pets must be kept on leash while in the park. Vehicles are restricted to authorized roadways or parking areas only. Off-road vehicles may not be operated on any of the government property at Navarro Mills Lake. Fires are restricted to designated areas only. There are no picnic sites in Liberty Hill Park. A camping permit must be purchased to occupy any of the sites. The   following fees are only charged to members of the public who have not paid the   standard camping fees. Boat Launch Fee $5.00 Swim Beach Fee $2.00 per person $5.00  per   vehicle Dump Station Fee $4.00 Pecan Point Park Located in north central Texas, Pecan Point Park is located on the north shore   of Navarro Mills Lake. Pecan Point Park offers 5 campsites with water and 50 amp   electric service, 30 sites without hookups, public restrooms and a dump station.   The park is open April 1 - September 30. For more detailed information, you may visit the Developed Camping Areas page. Important Information The   gatehouse is located at the park entrance and is open for business between 6:00   a.m. and 10:00 p.m. The gate attendants live on site and are available in cases   of emergencies 24 hours per day. All user fees are collected at the   gatehouse. Additional Restrictions Pets must be kept on leash while in the park. Vehicles are restricted to authorized roadways or parking areas only. Off-road vehicles may not be operated on any of the government property at Navarro Mills Lake. Fires are restricted to designated areas only. There are no picnic sites in Liberty Hill Park. A camping permit must be purchased to occupy any of the sites. The   following fees are only charged to members of the public who have not paid the   standard camping fees. Dump Station Fee $4.00 Brushie Prairie  Park Located in north central Texas, Brushie Prairie Park is a non-fee park open   year-round and located on the north shore of Navarro Mills Lake. Brushie Prairie   Park offers 10 picnic sites, a two lane boat ramp and a vault toilet restroom. Water and electric services are not provided in Brushie Prairie Park.
