Corps of Engineers Parks at Belton Lake

Cedar Ridge Park

Cedar Ridge Park is a campground located on the east side of the lake. Sanitary dump stations are available at each side of the park.

Live Oak Ridge Park

Live Oak Ridge Park is located on the east shore of the lake adjacent to the Belton Lake Dam. A sanitary dump station is conveniently located within the park.

Westcliff Park

Westcliff Park closes for the winter season from November 1 through March 1. The boat ramp is available for use year-round. The campground features a waterborne restroom with showers and a sanitary dump station.

White Flint Park

White Flint Park is peaceful campground located along the western shores on the upper part of Belton Lake. The park closes during the winter season from November 1 through March 1 but the park's boat ramp remains open year-round. Included in the park is a waterborne restroom with showers, a sanitary dump station conveniently located near the entrance to the park and a 2-lane boat ramp with restroom that is open to both campers and for day use.
