                14 Non-equestrian camping is allowed in the Big Pond Equestrian Camping Area. Maximum combination of people and horses is 8 per site. All sites have pull-off parking. Be prepared to use a "string line" to properly secure horses. Do not tie horses to trees or shrubs. Must dispose of manure and waste hay. Restrooms do not have hot water or showers. Horses entering park need a negative Coggins test less than 12 months old.                 0.8 These sites are in a flat area along the trail system, below a scenic overlook and near the west end of the dam on Lake Copper Breaks. Distance to campsites is about 3/4 mile. Water is not available in this area, and the sites do not have fire rings or grills. Ground fires are prohibited. Containerized stoves are allowed. Pets must be kept on no more than a 6-foot leash.                 8 Number of Sites:
                4 This group camp is in the Kiowa Camp Area. The site has four campsites; fee is $12 per campsite and up to eight people are allowed per site. Each campsite has a waist-high grill, and a large waist-high grill sits between two of the sites. The closest water sources are across the road in the day-use area and next to the Kiowa restroom. When not being used by a group, the four sites can be rented individually.
