Operator alerts — check before traveling

Burn Ban Oct. 29, 2026 - The park is under a burn ban. No wood or charcoal fires are allowed in the fire rings. For cooking, you may use wood smoker or pellet grills, propane or charcoal grills, cook stoves, griddles, or propane fire pits if they have a lid. Area Closures Sept. 18, 2026 - The boat ramp on the Hen House side of the park is closed. We expect it to reopen in the spring of 2027. The 100 and 200 camping loops, restrooms and fish cleaning station at the Hen House Ridge Unit are closed for a construction project through February 2027. The Hen House day-use area remains open. Holiday Hours The park will remain open throughout the holidays. The office will be closed during the following times: Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26: closing at noon Christmas Eve, Dec. 24: closing at noon Christmas Day, Dec. 25: closed all day New Year's Day, Jan. 1: open 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Check back for updates.
