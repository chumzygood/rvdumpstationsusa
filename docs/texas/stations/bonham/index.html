Holiday Hours The park will remain open throughout the holidays. The office will be closed during the following times: Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, 2026 - Closing at 10 a.m. Christmas Eve, Dec. 24, 2026 - Closing at 10 a.m. Christmas Day, Dec. 25, 2026 - Closed all day If you plan to visit during this time, please make reservations online or use the self-pay station at park headquarters. Hydrilla Alert Sept. 1, 2025 - The lake is infested with hydrilla, an invasive plant that creates large mats of vegetation across the surface of the water. We are working on controlling and removing the hydrilla, but it can affect swimming, fishing and boating. Please remember to always clean, drain and dry your boat! Check back for updates.
