Operator alerts — check before traveling

Screwworm Notice The park is within an infested zone for the new world screwworm . For the safety of animals and to prevent the spread of this dangerous pest: Horse inspection may be required before leaving the park. Please call park headquarters before visiting to ask about TAHC requirements and schedule an inspection. If you bring pets, inspect them daily for cuts, wounds and signs of infestation. If you see animals with unusual wounds or maggots, do not move them. Let someone know where you saw them: For pets/livestock : Contact park staff or the Animal Health Commission at (737) 900-7455. For wildlife : Contact park staff or call (512) 389-4505. Burn Ban Jan. 1, 2026 - No wood fires are allowed. However, charcoal and containerized fuel stoves (i.e. propane) are allowed for cooking. Primitive Area Closures The primitive camping area will be closed on the following dates: 6 a.m. on Oct. 2 to 8 a.m. on Oct. 10 6 a.m. on Nov. 6 to 8 a.m. on Nov. 11 6 a.m. on Feb. 19 to 8 a.m. on Feb. 24 Only permitted hunters will be allowed on these dates. Contact the park for more information. Check back for updates. Official alert ↗
