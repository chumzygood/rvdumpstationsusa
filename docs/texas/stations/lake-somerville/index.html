Operator alerts — check before traveling

Burn Ban Nov. 6, 2026 - The Birch Creek Unit of the park is under a burn ban. No wood fires are allowed. You may cook with charcoal in enclosed grills and containerized fuel stoves. Birch Creek Alerts Aug. 26, 2025 - Pavilions are closed. Feb. 16, 2024 - The fishing pier is closed due to damage. Hunt Closures Flag Pond Loop and Waldo's Loop trails will be closed from sunrise to noon daily during Early Teal, Regular North Zone Duck and Youth-Only Waterfowl seasons. Ask at headquarters if they are closed during your visit. Only hunters with permits will be allowed in closed areas. Unit Closures The Birch Creek Unit will be closed on the following dates: 4 p.m. on Dec. 1 to noon on Dec. 4 4 p.m. on Jan. 5 to noon on Jan. 8 The Nails Creek Unit will be closed on the following dates: 4 p.m. on Jan. 12 to 5 p.m. on Jan. 15 The Trailway will be closed on the following dates: 4 p.m. on Dec. 4 to 5 p.m. on Dec. 6 4 p.m. on Jan. 8 to 5 p.m. on Jan. 10 Only permitted hunters will be allowed on these dates. Contact the park for more information. Trailway Bridges Out May 1, 2024 - Two bridges on the Trailway are washed out, one over Nails Creek and one over Yegua Creek near the Newman Bottom parking area. Please reach out to either Nails Creek or Birch Creek headquarters for more information before heading out on the Trailway. Contact the park for more information or check back for updates.
