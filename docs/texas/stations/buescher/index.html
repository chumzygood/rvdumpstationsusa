Operator alerts — check before traveling

Screwworm Alert New world screwworm has been found in parts of Texas. Check your pets and livestock often. If you see unusual wounds or maggots, do not move the animal . Call the Texas Animal Health Commission at (800) 550‑8242 or contact a private veterinarian. For wildlife with suspicious wounds or maggots, notify park staff or call (512) 389‑4505. Official alert ↗

Burn Ban Oct. 19, 2026 - The park is under a burn ban. No wood fires allowed. You may use charcoal or containerized fuel stoves (i.e. propane) for cooking only. Low Lake Level June 22, 2026 - Buescher's lake is lower than normal. There is no view of or access to the lake from the cabins or from the Oak Haven and Lakeview campgrounds. Fishing is still available at the day-use picnic and canoe launch areas. Check back for updates. Official alert ↗
