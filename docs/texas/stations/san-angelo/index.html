Operator alerts — check before traveling

Screwworm Alert New world screwworm has been found in parts of Texas. Check your pets and livestock often. If you see unusual wounds or maggots, do not move the animal . Call the Texas Animal Health Commission at (800) 550‑8242 or contact a private veterinarian. For wildlife with suspicious wounds or maggots, notify park staff or call (512) 389‑4505. Official alert ↗

No Phones or Internet Nov. 9, 2026 - Phones and internet connections are down at the park. Call (325) 450-5755 from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. for assistance. Hunt Closures Some areas of the park will be closed for wildlife management activities on the following dates: Oct. 23-25, Nov. 20-22: All trails south of the Burkett Trailhead and the Isabel Harte and Red Arroyo camping areas will close at 5 p.m. the day before the first day of the hunt and will reopen at 8 a.m. the day after the hunt ends. The Red Arroyo boat ramp and all other roads on the south side of the park will be closed. The Lakeview (main) boat ramp will be open for waterfowl hunters, only. Nov. 3-5, Dec. 1-3: All trails north of the Burkett Trailhead and the North Concho and Riverbend camping areas will close at 5 p.m. the day before the first day of the hunt and will reopen at 2 p.m. the day the hunt ends. Jan. 12-14, 2027: All trails north and west of the Red Arroyo Camping Area, and Isabel Harte, North Concho and Riverbend camping areas will close at 5 p.m. the day before the first day of the hunt and will reopen at 2 p.m. the day the hunt ends. North Unit Gate Access Look for the gate code on your entry receipt or check in at the south gatehouse (362 S. FM 2288). Check back for updates or contact the park for more information.
