Operator alerts — check before traveling

Burn Bans Nov. 6, 2026 - The South Sulphur Unit is under a burn ban. No wood fires are allowed. Propane or containerized fuels may be used for cooking only. Boat Ramp Notice Nov. 6, 2025 - The Honey Creek boat ramp at the South Sulphur unit is closed due to the low lake level. The boat ramps at South Sulphur in Gulls Bluff, the Tira Satellite boat ramp, and Lone Pine boat ramp at Doctors Creek Unit remain open. Please be aware of low lake levels and use caution at the boat ramps. Trail Alert Trails close to horseback riders after rainfall. Check conditions before you come by calling the park. Park Closures The entire park will be closed on the following dates: 10 p.m. Jan. 8 to 8 a.m. Jan. 11 The Coyote Run trail at the South Sulphur unit will be closed (but equestrian trails will remain open): 10 p.m. Dec. 4 to 8 a.m. Dec. 21 Only permitted hunters will be allowed on these dates. Contact the park for more information. Check back for updates. Official alert ↗
