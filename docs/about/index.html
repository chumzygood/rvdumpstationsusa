ABOUT RV DUMP STATIONS USA

Sources, not assumptions.

An independent national directory starting with Texas. We help RV travelers find operator-listed waste-disposal facilities and see what is—and is not—confirmed.

What we verify

Every property must have location-specific evidence from its official operator or government agency explicitly listing a dump station. Texas state parks come from TPWD’s public amenity records, supplemented by park pages and alerts. USACE campground listings use the agency’s park descriptions. Travel centers use the operator’s location-specific amenities and addresses.

What we do not assume

We have not inspected these sites. A listed amenity does not establish live service, non-guest access, potable water, payment methods or large-rig clearance. Store, park or office hours are not dump-station hours. Campsite length limits and water/sewer hookups are not proof of dump-station access or a potable fill. Unconfirmed facts stay unknown.

Locations and counting

One listing per property, not per dump lane. Where a USACE source identifies the lake but no verified street address, we show a named lake area and link directly to operator directions; we never reuse the project-office address as the campground address. Cities and lake areas are browse groups, not radius searches. We do not claim a comprehensive inventory.

Closures and updates

Official alerts and restrictions are displayed on station pages. A last-checked date records a source review, not an on-site inspection or confirmation of service today. Review the operator page before traveling.

Independence and sister project

RV Dump Stations USA is a sister project to TossMap, the disposal and recycling directory. We are not affiliated with listed agencies or operators. There are no paid placements, sponsors or affiliate arrangements. Any future featured placements would be clearly labeled and would not alter factual verification.

Coverage

Texas is the first published state. Other states are marked coming soon without empty landing pages. We do not scrape Google Maps or copy third-party directory listings.
